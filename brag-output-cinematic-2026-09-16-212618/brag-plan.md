# Brag Plan (Cinematic variant) — Net Swiss Knife

Same product research as the app-store version. This file overrides tone,
storyboard, and audio only.

## 9-question rubric (deltas)

6. **Tone.** Preset: **cinematic**. Creative direction: "the network as an
   unseen world, this app as the instrument that reveals it" — trailer-scale
   drama, epic short declaratives, dramatic scale-in reveals (0.95 → 1.0),
   full-bleed type.
7. **Audio.** `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
   (steady/clean, per `audio.md`'s tone table — also the cinematic pick),
   with a low swell built via a volume automation curve rather than a flat
   bed, plus 2-3 big accent hits: `impact/impactBell_heavy_000` for the
   hero reveal, `impact/impactBell_heavy_003` for the outro slam.
8. **Share caption (cinematic variant):**
   "In every network, there are things unseen. Net Swiss Knife: fourteen
   tools built to find them."

## Storyboard (target ~25s, 6 scenes, dramatic scale-in reveals)

**1. Hook — 0.00–4.00s (4.0s)**
Full-bleed dark. Epic statement, scale-in from 0.95: **"In every network,
there are things unseen."**

**2. Reveal — 4.00–8.00s (4.0s)**
Home screen grid, dramatic push-in (0.95→1.0). Line: **"One app finds them
all."**

**3. Capabilities — 8.00–13.00s (5.0s, 5 items, ~1.0s each)**
Superpowers stated short and declarative, one tool-screen flash per line,
dramatic crossfade + scale between each: Traceroute, Port Scanner, TLS
Inspector, DNS Lookup, WHOIS Lookup.
```
Every hop, traced.
Every port, probed.
Every certificate, verified.
Every record, resolved.
Every domain, uncovered.
```

**4. Highlight — Subnet Calculator — 13.00–17.50s (4.5s)**
**"Binary, decoded in an instant."**

**5. Highlight — Ping — 17.50–21.50s (4.0s)**
**"Real numbers. Real time."**

**6. Outro — 21.50–25.00s (3.5s)**
**"NET SWISS KNIFE"** slams full-screen (scale-in, not a hard cut).
Tagline underneath. Music swell implied via a volume automation peak
timed to the slam.

Six scenes is one over the tone's "4-5" guideline, kept deliberately for
parity with the other three cuts' tool-breadth requirement — the
Capabilities scene folds five tools into one beat rather than skipping
them, consistent with cinematic's "highlight style: capabilities stated
like superpowers," not a montage.

## Audio

- Music: vol-12 at a rising volume curve — low under the hook (0.18),
  building through the reveal/capabilities scenes (0.3), swelling toward
  the outro (0.4) before a slow fade.
- SFX: `impact/impactBell_heavy_000` under the Reveal scene's push-in
  (the "hero moment"), `impact/impactBell_heavy_003` on the outro slam.
  Nothing else — cinematic restraint means 2 big hits, not a layer of
  clicks.

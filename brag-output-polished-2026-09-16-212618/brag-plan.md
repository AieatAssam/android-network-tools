# Brag Plan (Polished variant) — Net Swiss Knife

Same product research as the app-store version. This file overrides tone,
storyboard, and audio only.

## 9-question rubric (deltas)

6. **Tone.** Preset: **polished**. Creative direction: "a quiet, premium
   product film — the tool is confident enough to not shout." Restraint
   as the creative choice: slow crossfades, generous letter-spacing,
   mixed-case type, no bullets, no per-tap SFX spam.
7. **Audio.** Sparse: one steady, clean music bed
   (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, per
   `audio.md`'s tone table — "steady and clean… best for polished,
   cinematic") at a normal-but-restrained bed volume, plus only 2 quiet
   accent cues total (hook settle, outro). No per-card taps.
8. **Share caption (polished variant):**
   "Introducing Net Swiss Knife: fourteen network tools, one calm
   interface. Traceroute, port scanning, DNS, TLS inspection, live
   diagnostics — all in your pocket, with nothing else asking for your
   attention."

## Storyboard (target ~24.5s, 5 scenes, slow 0.6-0.8s crossfades)

**1. Hook — 0.00–4.00s (4.0s)**
Full-scale product mark: icon + "Net Swiss Knife" wordmark, generous
letter-spacing, slow fade-up (no snap/punch). Tagline settles beneath a
beat later: "Your all-in-one Android networking toolkit."

**2. Tool sequence — 4.00–11.70s (7.70s, 7 items, ~1.1s each)**
One phone card at a time, each **slow-crossfading** (0.6-0.8s) into the
next — never a hard cut. Same 7 tools as the other cuts (Traceroute, Port
Scanner, DNS Lookup, Wi-Fi Spectrum, TLS Inspector, mDNS Browser, WHOIS),
each with one small, quiet caption line — no bullets, no stacked lists.
This is "one feature per scene" repeated calmly rather than a rapid
montage: the polished law is restraint in the *transition*, not fewer
tools shown.

**3. Highlight — Subnet Calculator — 11.70–16.20s (4.5s)**
Single feature, full attention: the binary breakdown card, slow push-in,
one caption line: "Binary, decoded."

**4. Highlight — Ping — 16.20–20.50s (4.3s)**
The completed live run against 8.8.8.8: RTT chart, one caption line:
"Real diagnostics, quietly delivered."

**5. Outro — 20.50–24.50s (4.0s)**
Product name. Tagline. Hold. No slam, no hard cut — just a slow fade to
black under a trailing quiet chime.

## Audio

- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` at 0.28
  volume (steady bed, no ducking needed).
- SFX: exactly two — a soft settle chime at the hook (`interface/bong_001`
  at low volume) and a single quiet outro accent
  (`impact/impactSoft_medium_000`). No per-card click/drop sounds during
  the tool sequence — the crossfades carry all the motion; polished tone
  gets there through restraint, not marked absence of sound.
- No beat-locking attempted for this tone — "confidence through slow
  reveals" is about calm pacing, not hitting transient hits.

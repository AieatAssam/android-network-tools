## 9-question rubric (deltas from the app-store version)

6. **Tone.** Preset: **chaotic**. Creative direction: "your network is a crime
   scene, this app is the whole forensics team" — treat 14 tools like a
   rapid-fire arsenal reveal, ALL CAPS stat-dump energy, hard cuts, zoom
   punches. Still shows real product (the chaotic law is about energy, not
   about faking claims).
7. **Audio.** Dense: SFX on nearly every beat, `impact/impactPunch_heavy_*`
   + `interface/glitch_002/004` + `casino/dice-throw-*` mixed. Music:
   `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (compact, punchy
   60s loop, 110 BPM) — per `audio.md`'s tone table, vol-10 is the chaotic
   pick.
8. **Share caption (chaotic variant):**
   "YOUR NETWORK IS A CRIME SCENE. Net Swiss Knife is the whole forensics
   team — 14 tools, one app, 0% packet loss. No accounts. No ads. No mercy."

## Storyboard (target ~18.5s, 7 scenes, beat-locked)

Track: vol-10, beat grid ~0.55s spacing (110 BPM). Strong cues in window:
15.82s, 18.01s, 18.55s, 20.19s, 20.74s, 21.83s, 22.37s, 22.92s, 23.46s, 24.01s.
Every scene boundary below is snapped to a beat-grid point; the two
outro-adjacent boundaries (15.82s, 18.55s) land on **strong cues**.

**1. Hook A — 0.00–1.37s (1.37s)** — beat-grid: 0.00, 1.37
Full-bleed flash: **"NETWORKS ARE CHAOS."** — huge, tilted, white on near-black,
hard cut in, no ease-in fade.

**2. Hook B — 1.37–2.46s (1.09s)** — beat-grid: 1.37, 2.46
Flash cut (white frame) → **"WE BROUGHT A KNIFE."** — green accent, zoom-punch
in (scale 1.2→1.0).

**3. Reveal — 2.46–6.28s (3.82s)** — beat-grid: 2.46, 6.28
`home_grid.png` zoom-punches in off a flash cut. Stamp overlay:
**"14 TOOLS. ONE APP."** in oversized caps, slightly tilted, over the phone.

**4. Highlight 1 (Wi-Fi) — 6.28–9.29s (3.01s)** — beat-grid: 6.28, 9.29
`wifi_spectrum.png` zoom-punch. One-line stat stamp: **"LIVE SPECTRUM. PER
CHANNEL."**

**5. Highlight 2 (Subnet) — 9.29–12.56s (3.27s)** — beat-grid: 9.29, 12.56
`subnet_binary.png` zoom-punch. Stat stamp: **"BINARY. DECODED INSTANTLY."**

**6. Highlight 3 (Ping) — 12.56–15.82s (3.26s)** — beat-grid: 12.56,
15.82 (strong cue)
`ping_live.png` → hard cut to `ping_chart.png` mid-scene. Stat stamp:
**"0% PACKET LOSS."**

**7. Outro — 15.82–18.55s (2.73s)** — beat-locked: 15.82s (strong cue,
scene start) and 18.55s (strong cue, final hit)
Cut to black. **"NET SWISS KNIFE"** slams in full-bleed at 15.82s. Tagline
**"No accounts. No ads. Just tools."** stamps under it. Hard cut to black at
18.55s on the strong cue.

`// beat-locked`):

14 network tools in one app. Open source; no ads, account or analytics.

Ping, traceroute, port and LAN scanners, Wi-Fi, speed test, DNS, WHOIS, TLS, HTTP, subnet, mDNS, Wake-on-LAN and SNMP topology in Material You.

━━ TOOLS ━━

PING — ICMP RTT, packet loss and min/avg/max chart with reachability fallback. Configure count, timeout, payload, TTL and interval; continuous mode exports CSV.

TRACEROUTE — hops show per-probe RTT and min/average/max stats; reverse DNS and approximate location fill in when available. ICMP or UDP, up to 64 hops, 1–5 probes, automatic MTU discovery.

PORT SCANNER — TCP reachability, service names and banners. Six presets or a custom range up to 10,000 ports.

LAN SCANNER — discover devices by subnet with IP, hostname, MAC/vendor, open ports, response time and gateway flag; search and filter results.

WI-FI SCANNER — group access points by SSID; inspect signal, channel, width, security and standard by band (2.4/5/6 GHz), with a best-channel tip.

SPEED TEST — live Cloudflare latency, download and upload gauges/charts; reports jitter and peak/average throughput.

DNS LOOKUP — ten record types (A, AAAA, MX, TXT, CNAME, NS, SOA, PTR, SRV, CAA) against System, Google, Cloudflare, OpenDNS, Quad9 or a custom server. Shows actual RR types, response code, flags, sections, server used and raw response; PTR accepts a plain IP.

WHOIS LOOKUP — domains, IPv4, IPv6 and ASNs. Auto tries RDAP first, then falls back to classic WHOIS; choose RDAP or WHOIS directly. Parsed registrar, dates, name servers, DNSSEC and status codes in plain English.

TLS INSPECTOR — the server-presented certificate chain for any TCP host: subject, issuer, validity, SANs, algorithms and SHA-256 fingerprint, plus TLS version, ALPN, cipher suite and trust status. Checks hostname, expiry, chain and weak keys/signatures; optional protocol probes, pin comparison and PEM sharing.

HTTP PROBE — GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE, HEAD and OPTIONS with custom headers, a body editor and per-hop redirect details. HTTPS-to-HTTP redirects are blocked; cross-origin body replay asks first. Overview, Headers and Body tabs, plus security-header ratings.

SUBNET CALCULATOR — network, broadcast, first and last host and host counts from CIDR, mask or IP range. Colour-coded binary breakdown of network and host bits, conversion between CIDR, wildcard, hex and binary masks, plus class and scope.

mDNS BROWSER — discover Bonjour / DNS-SD services on your LAN without knowing what to look for: AirPlay, printers, HomeKit, Spotify Connect and more, with hostname, port, IP addresses and TXT records.

WAKE-ON-LAN — wake a sleeping machine with a magic packet. Accepts every common MAC format, with custom broadcast address and port.

NETWORK TOPOLOGY — SNMP v1/v2c/v3 discovery walking LLDP and CDP neighbours from a seed device. Per-node vendor, model, firmware and uptime, interface status and VLANs, on an interactive pan-and-zoom map.

━━ DESIGN ━━

Material 3 with dynamic colour, light/dark themes, streamed results, pinned tools and configurable defaults.

━━ PRIVACY ━━

No account, ads, analytics or usage tracking. Results stay on your device. Requests go to the target or service needed by the selected tool: DNS resolver; IANA/rdap.org/registry or WHOIS servers; ipinfo.io for traceroute IPv4 hops that pass the app's local filter; and speed.cloudflare.com for synthetic test traffic. Independent from and not endorsed by Cloudflare, Inc.

Android requires location permission for Wi-Fi scans; physical location is not recorded or transmitted.
